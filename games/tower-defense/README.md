# Tower Defense

Strategic tower defense with multiple tower types and enemy variants. Progress through 5 levels, 20 waves each!

## How to Play

- Press 1-4 to select tower type
- Click anywhere on the canvas to place selected tower
- Click on placed towers to select them
- Press U to upgrade selected tower
- Press S to sell selected tower
- Stop enemies before they reach the red square
- Complete all 5 levels (100 waves total) for ultimate victory

## Level Progression

- Each level has 20 waves
- After completing 20 waves, advance to next level
- **70% of your gold carries over** between levels (+50 bonus)
- **Towers reset** each level (plan your economy!)
- Enemies get tougher with each level
- Gain +5 lives at the start of each level
- Wave bonuses are smaller, so spend wisely!

## Tower Types

- Basic (50g): Balanced damage and range
- Sniper (100g): Long range, high damage, slow fire rate
- Rapid (75g): Fast fire rate, shorter range, lower damage
- Splash (120g): Area damage, hits multiple enemies

## Enemy Types

- Normal: Standard speed and health
- Fast: Quick but fragile
- Tank: Slow but heavily armored
- Boss: Appears every 10 enemies on wave 5+

## Strategy

- Save gold between levels for stronger starts
- Mix tower types for effectiveness
- Upgrade towers at key choke points strategically
- Each level increases enemy difficulty
- Splash towers are great for groups
- Sniper towers handle bosses well
- Plan ahead - your towers reset each level!

## Controls

- 1-4: Select tower type
- Mouse Click: Place tower or select existing tower
- U: Upgrade selected tower
- S: Sell selected tower

## Scoring

- Earn gold for each enemy destroyed
- Wave bonus: 25 + (wave × 2) gold
- 70% of gold carries to next level
- Upgrade towers strategically - resources are limited!
