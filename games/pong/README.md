# Pong

A classic arcade game built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas featuring AI opponent gameplay.

## How to Play

- Control your paddle to hit the ball past the AI opponent
- First to reach 10 points wins
- Ball speeds up with each paddle hit

## Controls

- **W/S keys** or **Arrow Up/Down**: Move your paddle up/down

## Scoring

- Score when opponent misses the ball
- Win condition: First to 10 points

## Features

- Three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard)
- Smart AI opponent with increasing speed
- Dynamic ball physics with angle-based rebounds
- Progressive ball speed increase
- Real-time score tracking
- Win/lose conditions
- Clean retro aesthetic

## Difficulty Levels

- **Easy**: Slower ball and AI (default)
- **Medium**: Faster ball and AI for more challenge
- **Hard**: Maximum speed for expert players

## Tech Stack

- Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
- HTML5 Canvas
- CSS3
