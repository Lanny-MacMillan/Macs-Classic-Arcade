# Vampire Survivor

A fast-paced survival game where you fight endless waves of enemies while leveling up and gaining powerful upgrades.

## How to Play

- **Movement**: WASD or Arrow Keys
- **Attacking**: Automatic - targets nearest enemy
- **Goal**: Survive as long as possible

## Features

- Auto-targeting weapon system
- XP collection and leveling
- Upgrade selection on level up
- Progressively harder difficulty
- Health and XP bars

## Upgrades

- Damage increase
- Fire rate boost
- Movement speed
- Max HP increase
- Projectile range
- Healing

## Tips

- Keep moving to avoid enemy swarms
- Pick up XP orbs quickly
- Balance offense and defense upgrades
- Watch your health bar
