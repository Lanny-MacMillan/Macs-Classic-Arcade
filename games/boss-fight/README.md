# Boss Fight

An intense arcade boss battle game with 5 escalating levels where you face increasingly powerful guardians.

## How to Play

- Use **WASD** or **Arrow Keys** to move your character
- Press **SPACE** to shoot projectiles at the boss
- Dodge incoming attack patterns
- Defeat each boss to advance to the next level

## Level Progression

**Level 1: Guardian Awakened**

- Health: 300
- Weapon: Standard Shot
- Difficulty: Beginner

**Level 2: Guardian Enraged**

- Health: 450
- Weapon: Standard Shot (Faster)
- Difficulty: Moderate

**Level 3: Guardian Empowered**

- Health: 600
- Weapon: Double Shot + Rapid Fire
- Difficulty: Hard

**Level 4: Guardian Ascended**

- Health: 750
- Weapon: Double Shot + Faster Fire
- Difficulty: Expert

**Level 5: Guardian Supreme**

- Health: 1000
- Weapon: Triple Shot + Maximum Fire Rate
- Difficulty: Ultimate

## Player Upgrades

- **Rapid Fire**: Decreases cooldown between shots
- **Double Shot**: Fire 2 projectiles simultaneously
- **Triple Shot**: Fire 3 projectiles simultaneously

## Attack Patterns

The boss cycles through five distinct attack patterns:

- **Spread**: Fan of projectiles aimed downward
- **Spiral**: Rotating circular burst
- **Wave**: Horizontal wave with sinusoidal movement
- **Cross**: Projectiles fired in cardinal and diagonal directions
- **Circle**: Complete 360-degree burst

Each level increases pattern frequency and projectile speed.

## Victory Condition

Defeat all 5 guardians to achieve ultimate victory!
