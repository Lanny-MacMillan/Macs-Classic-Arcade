# Color Switch

A challenging arcade game where you must navigate through rotating obstacles by matching your color with the correct segments.

## How to Play

- Click or press SPACE to make your player jump
- Pass through obstacle segments that match your color
- Collect color switchers to change your player's color
- Avoid touching segments that don't match your color
- Score points by passing through obstacles

## Game Mechanics

- **Player**: A colored ball that falls with gravity
- **Obstacles**: Rotating circles divided into 4 colored segments
- **Color Switchers**: Small rotating icons that change your player's color
- **Scoring**: Earn 1 point for each obstacle passed

## Controls

- **Click / SPACE**: Jump upward
- **Back Button**: Return to arcade menu

## Strategy Tips

- Time your jumps carefully to align with matching colors
- Grab color switchers strategically when approaching difficult obstacles
- The obstacles rotate continuously, so plan your path ahead
- Keep momentum steady to maintain better control
